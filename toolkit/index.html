Curated & Used by: Sennagraphy

Introduction

After completing my 45-Day Researcher Journey, I realized something important: research quality is not determined by intelligence alone, but by systems and tools used consistently over time.

During my journey, I tested many platforms — some stayed, many were discarded. This toolkit is not a collection of random tools, but a battle-tested research system I actively use to analyze Web3 fundamentals, on-chain behavior, narratives, risks, and opportunities.

Every tool below earned its place through real research use cases, mistakes, validation, and cross-checking.

Phase 1: Fundamentals, Metrics & On-Chain Reality

Understanding protocol health through real blockchain data. When analyzing any protocol, I always start with on-chain truth, not narratives.

Tools I Rely On

  • Dune Analytics — custom SQL queries and dashboards for deep DeFi protocol analysis. Lets me visualize real-time protocol metrics. X
  • DeFiLlama — tracks TVL, capital flows, and ecosystem growth across chains. Ideal for quick comparisons. X
  • Token Terminal — revenue, fees, and economic sustainability metrics for protocols. Shows which protocols generate real cash flow. X
  • Nansen — analyzes wallet behavior and smart money activity. Useful for spotting trends and high-value holders. X
  • Artemis — chain-level metrics and comparative analytics. Helps evaluate performance across multiple networks. X
  • Footprint Analytics — structured on-chain data visualization with user-friendly dashboards. X
  • L2Beat — Layer-2 risk frameworks, adoption metrics, and TVL breakdowns. X
  • Bitcoin Layers — insight into Bitcoin scaling layers and ecosystem developments. X
  • CryptoQuant — exchange flows, miner activity, and macro on-chain signals. X
  • Glassnode — advanced on-chain indicators for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other assets. X
  • CoinGlass — derivatives, liquidation, and leverage data for market risk assessment. X
  • EigenPhi — DeFi transaction paths and MEV insights. Great for tracing fund movement. X

Key Lesson

  • On-chain data removes bias. If the data disagrees with the narrative, the narrative is wrong.

Final Lessons

  • Verify on-chain first — data > narrative. Insights are only as strong as the truth on-chain.
  • Tools support thinking, they don’t replace it — frameworks and judgment are more important than access to any single platform.
  • Risk is as important as reward — yield, hype, or early opportunities must be evaluated through measurable risk.
  • Narratives move faster than fundamentals — tracking attention, sentiment, and market behavior allows you to anticipate, not just react.
  • Consistency beats raw intelligence — repeated use of structured systems and workflows ensures higher quality research over time.
  • Primary sources are non-negotiable — verifying at the blockchain layer, reading protocols’ code, or tracking official data is the only way to truly know.

Closing

This toolkit is more than a collection of tools — it’s a reflection of how I think, analyze, and validate ideas as a Web3 researcher.

It’s built from real-world testing, mistakes, iteration, and validation. Every platform here earned its place through repeated use, data-driven insights, and cross-verification. More than just a resource list, it represents a research framework that can be applied to uncover opportunities, assess risks, and understand the ecosystem with clarity.

Research is not about knowing every tool — it’s about building systems, asking the right questions, and drawing conclusions from verified information.

This system evolves alongside the Web3 ecosystem, just as my thinking evolves through each new project, narrative, or market cycle. Use it as a guide, adapt it, and remember: data without judgment is noise, and tools without process are meaningless.
