Next-Level Researcher Toolkit 🔧

Curated by: Sennagraphy

Introduction

After completing my 45-Day Researcher Journey, I realized that strong research is not only about knowledge, but also about having the right tools. The Next-Level Researcher Toolkit is a curated collection of Web3 research, analytics, and intelligence tools that I personally use to analyze fundamentals, on-chain data, market structure, narratives, tokenomics, and ecosystem activity.

This toolkit reflects how I approach research in practice: starting from high-level fundamentals, diving into on-chain data, validating narratives through social signals, and cross-checking everything with market and trading data. Each tool listed here has been tested and documented through public X threads.

How to Use This Toolkit

  • Begin with fundamentals and on-chain metrics to understand real protocol activity.
  • Validate insights using market and trading data.
  • Track narratives and sentiment before price reactions.
  • Use discovery platforms to find emerging opportunities early.
  • Combine multiple tools to reduce bias and improve accuracy.

Toolkit Categories & Purpose

Fundamentals, Metrics, & On-Chain Data Visualization

Used to analyze protocol health, capital flows, user activity, and on-chain behavior. These tools form the foundation of data-driven Web3 research.

Market Data

Provides price, market capitalization, volume, and historical context to compare assets and validate whether on-chain growth is reflected in market performance.

Social & Sentiment Analytics

Tracks narratives, attention, and community sentiment to identify emerging trends and shifts in market psychology.

Trading Data

Focuses on liquidity, DEX volume, and short-term market behavior to understand how traders interact with tokens and pools.

Tokenomics

Analyzes token supply schedules, unlock events, and emission structures to assess long-term sustainability and dilution risk.

Discovery

Surfaces new protocols, ecosystems, and investment opportunities before they reach mainstream awareness.

Research & Reports

Provides structured research, sector analysis, and institutional-grade insights to support deeper understanding of the crypto market.

Blockchain Explorers (Core Infrastructure)

Used to verify transactions, wallets, contracts, and on-chain events at the most fundamental level.
